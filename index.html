About & MRV Method

Measurement, Reporting & Verification (MRV)

  • Measure: Track intake mass (agricultural waste), moisture content (%), and product mass per batch using calibrated scales and moisture meters.
  • Report: Weekly batch sheets documenting input waste, biochar yield, and operational parameters. Monthly summaries consolidated into CSV format for transparency.
  • Verify: Random lab testing (proximate/ultimate analysis) to measure fixed carbon content. Stability factor applied to adjust sequestered CO₂ conservatively.
  • Conservative Accounting: All CO₂ estimates use a stability factor (default 0.8) pending lab calibration, ensuring underestimation rather than overstatement of impact.
  • Formulas: Biochar = Waste × Yield Factor; CO₂ (raw) = Biochar × tCO₂/t; CO₂ (stable) = CO₂ (raw) × Stability Factor
  • Transparency: All data, formulas, and assumptions are documented and available for third-party audit. CSV exports include per-hub breakdowns.
