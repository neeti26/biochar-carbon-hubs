# 🌱 Biochar Carbon Hubs: Decentralizing Decarbonization

This project proposes **Biochar Carbon Hubs** – decentralized, community-scale facilities that transform agricultural waste into biochar using **low-cost pyrolysis kilns**.

## 🔥 Problem
- **Industrial Emissions**: Power & Steel industries contribute ~80% of India’s industrial CO₂ emissions.  
- **Crop Residue Burning**: ~250 million tons of agri-waste burned annually → greenhouse gases + severe air pollution.  

## 💡 Solution
Biochar Carbon Hubs convert waste into biochar, achieving **dual impact**:  
1. **Soil Enrichment** – improves fertility, boosts water retention, and cuts fertilizer use by 30–40%.  
2. **Industrial Decarbonization** – replaces 10–20% of coke in steel furnaces and co-fires in power plants.  

Each hub processes **500 tons of waste annually**, producing **300 tons biochar** and avoiding **750 tons of CO₂ emissions**.
