# Solution Description

Our solution: **Biochar Carbon Hubs** – decentralised, community-scale hubs that transform agricultural waste into biochar using low-cost pyrolysis kilns.

## Dual Impact
1. **Soil Enrichment** → Biochar locks carbon for centuries, improves soil fertility, reduces fertilizer costs by 30–40%, and enhances water retention.  
2. **Industrial Decarbonisation** → Biochar replaces coal/coke in steel furnaces and serves as biomass fuel in power plants, cutting fossil dependency.

## Why It’s Unique
- **Carbon-negative** process, permanently sequestering CO₂.  
- **Affordable & scalable** technology deployable in villages.  
- **Women-led hubs** operated by SHGs, creating 15–20 green jobs per hub.  
- **Revenue model** based on biochar sales + carbon credits.
