# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Pilot & Prototype
- Establish prototype pyrolysis kiln.  
- Run a pilot Biochar Hub with a local farming community or FPO.  

## Phase 2: Industry Partnerships
- Collaborate with local steel/power industries for biochar testing in furnaces and boilers.  

## Phase 3: Scale & Pipeline
- Expand to 100 hubs.  
- Build a robust carbon credit trading pipeline.  

## Phase 4: National Roll-out
- Establish 10,000 hubs across India.  
- Position India as a global biochar leader.
