# Problem Statement

India faces a **dual crisis**:

1. **Industrial Emissions**  
The Power and Steel industries account for **80% of India’s industrial CO₂ emissions**, locking the nation into a fossil-heavy growth path.

2. **Agricultural Residue Burning**  
India produces ~250 million tons of crop residue every year, a significant portion of which is **burned in fields**, releasing greenhouse gases and creating severe air pollution.

This combination accelerates climate change, damages public health, and wastes a valuable resource stream.
