# Impact Analysis

## Per Hub (Annual)
- 500 tons agri waste processed  
- 300 tons biochar produced  
- 750 tons CO₂ emissions avoided  
- 15–20 rural jobs created  

## National Scale (10,000 Hubs)
- 5 million tons of agri waste processed annually  
- 3 million tons of biochar produced  
- 7.5 million tons of CO₂ emissions avoided annually  
- 150,000–200,000 rural jobs created  

## Comparisons
Avoided CO₂ is equivalent to removing **1.5 million cars from the road annually**.
