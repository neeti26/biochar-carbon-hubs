HOW TO USE:

Step 1: UPLOAD SOIL IMAGE
   • Click the upload area OR drag & drop
   • Supports: JPG, PNG (max 5MB)
   • Image appears as preview

Step 2: ANALYZE
   • Click "Analyze Soil" button
   • AI processes image (2-second simulation)
   • Status badge shows "Analyzing..." with pulse

Step 3: VIEW RESULTS
   System detects 4 possible soil types:
   
   📊 Clay-rich soil
      - High biochar suitability
      - Recommends 5-10 tons/hectare
      - +15-25% yield improvement
      - Adjusts: Yield Factor → 0.65, CO₂ → 2.7
   
   🏖️ Sandy soil
      - Very High suitability
      - Recommends 8-12 tons/hectare
      - +25-40% water retention
      - Adjusts: Yield Factor → 0.70, CO₂ → 2.8
   
   🌱 Loamy soil
      - Medium-High suitability
      - Recommends 3-6 tons/hectare
      - +20-30% microbial activity
      - Adjusts: Yield Factor → 0.60, CO₂ → 2.5
   
   ⚠️ Degraded/eroded soil
      - Critical suitability
      - Recommends 10-15 tons/hectare URGENT
      - Maximum carbon storage potential
      - Adjusts: Yield Factor → 0.75, CO₂ → 3.0, Stability → 0.85

Step 4: APPLY RECOMMENDATIONS
   • Click "Apply AI Recommendations to Calculator"
   • System automatically adjusts:
     - Yield Factor slider
     - CO₂ per ton slider
     - Stability Factor (if needed)
   • KPIs recalculate instantly
   • Page scrolls to top to show new metrics

TROUBLESHOOTING:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Upload area not clickable?
A: Ensure JavaScript is enabled. Try drag & drop instead.

Q: Image too large error?
A: Resize image to under 5MB using any photo editor.

Q: Analysis stuck on "Analyzing..."?
A: Refresh page and try again. Check browser console (F12).

Q: Recommendations not applying?
A: Make sure sliders are visible and not at min/max limits.
